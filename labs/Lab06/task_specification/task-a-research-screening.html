Task guidelines

Reference while you work

Help identify empirical studies evaluating home-based digital interventions for older adults.

Your role
Research assistant
Unit
One article: title and abstract
How to annotate
  1. Read the article’s title and abstract.
  2. Evaluate each inclusion criterion separately.
  3. Record Yes or No for every criterion.
  4. Select an overall decision: Include or Exclude.
  5. Add a short comment if needed.

Base decisions on the title and abstract. Do not search for additional information. Specialist questions can be referred to a senior reviewer.

Inclusion criteria
C1 · Older adult population

The study investigates adults aged 65 years or older. Include studies explicitly involving people aged 65+; exclude studies involving only younger participants.

C2 · Digital intervention

The study evaluates an intervention delivered or supported through digital technology, such as smartphone apps, web applications, digital coaching systems or wearable-supported interventions. Include digital interventions; exclude studies using technology only to collect research measurements.

C3 · Home-based intervention

The intervention is intended to be used in participants’ homes rather than exclusively in a hospital, clinic or laboratory. Include interventions explicitly described as home-based; exclude interventions explicitly delivered only in clinical facilities.

C4 · Empirical evaluation

The study reports an evaluation involving participants or other original empirical data. Include experimental, observational and empirical feasibility studies; exclude editorials, purely conceptual proposals and opinion pieces.

Overall screening decision

Include: The article appears suitable for the review.

Exclude: The article does not appear suitable for the review.

Use your judgment based on the available information.

Training examples
Example 1 · Include

Home-based smartphone exercise coaching for older adults

This study evaluated an eight-week smartphone-supported home exercise program involving 80 participants aged 70 years and older. The results describe adherence, usability and changes in physical activity.

Expected decision: Include. The study describes an empirical evaluation of a digital intervention designed for older adults and used at home.

Example 2 · Exclude

Clinical rehabilitation following knee surgery in middle-aged adults

This study evaluated a supervised rehabilitation program delivered in a hospital outpatient clinic to participants aged 40–55 years.

Expected decision: Exclude. The population does not meet the age criterion, and the intervention is not home-based or digital.

Questions & exceptions

Use the available title and abstract. If needed, add a short comment to explain your judgment. Specialist questions can be referred to a senior reviewer.
